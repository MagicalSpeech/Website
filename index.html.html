What We Offer

Therapy that transforms

We offer a full range of speech pathology services tailored to your unique needs — delivered with warmth, expertise, and genuine care.

🗣️

Speech & Articulation

Helping children and adults produce sounds clearly and confidently, so every word lands as intended.

🧠

Language Development

Supporting vocabulary, grammar, comprehension and expression for children from toddlers to teens.

🌊

Fluency (Stuttering)

Evidence-based strategies for fluent, confident communication — without fear or avoidance.

💡

AAC

Augmentative and Alternative Communication support for individuals who need additional tools to communicate effectively.

🤝

Social & Play Skills

Supporting the development of social interaction, play, and communication skills for children and adults with complex needs.

📖

Phonological Awareness & Literacy

Targeted intervention for reading, writing and spelling difficulties to build strong literacy foundations.

👂

Auditory Processing

Assessment and therapy for listening skills, helping clients process and understand sound more effectively.

💻

Telehealth Sessions

Flexible online therapy from the comfort of your home — just as effective, far more convenient.

✅

NDIS Supports

As a registered NDIS provider, we support participants to access the speech pathology services they need to thrive.

About the Practice

Locally owned,
genuinely caring

At Magical Speech Pathology we love to enrich our clients' lives with magical moments and learning experiences. Our clients create their own magic in their progress!

With more than 10 years of clinical experience, Magical Speech Pathology tailors individual therapy plans to each client in order to achieve the best outcome. We are committed to ongoing professional development and regularly engage in learning opportunities to provide the best service possible. We go above and beyond what is required and truly care about our clients' wellbeing, safety and success in their everyday lives.

★

Certified Practising Speech Pathologists — Our dedicated clinicians hold CPSP certification and pursue ongoing postgraduate professional education.

★

Members of Speech Pathology Australia (SPA) — All clinicians are proud members, upholding the highest professional standards.

★

Neurodiversity-affirming practice — We celebrate difference and support communication in all its forms.

★

Locally owned and operated — Proudly serving Newcastle, Lake Macquarie and Maitland communities.
